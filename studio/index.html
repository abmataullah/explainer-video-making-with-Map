1 What is the video about?

Writes the script, records the voice and renders the MP4. About 3 to 6 minutes. You can open the scenes afterwards, fix anything and render again.
or go step by step

🎧 Make the video from my own recording

Use any recording: ElevenLabs, AI Studio in the browser, or your own voice. No Gemini voice needed.
Your words are never changed. The studio matches every word of your script to the recording (forced alignment on your graphics card) to cut the scenes and time the captions.

2 Scenes 

  

    
    

  

  
Type countries by name in Bangla or English, separated by commas: বাংলাদেশ, মিয়ানমার or Bangladesh, Myanmar.
    Colours: লাল/red, কমলা/amber, সবুজ/green, নীল/blue, ফিরোজা/teal, বেগুনি/violet, হলুদ/yellow, গোলাপি/pink, ধূসর/grey.
    Arrows, one per line: চীন > মিয়ানমার : পাইপলাইন. Points, one per line: চট্টগ্রাম, 22.33, 91.83 (name, lat, lon).
