GEO EXPLAINER STUDIO

Start:  double-click GeoExplainer.exe  (opens http://localhost:7870)
        It keeps running in the background until you press Quit or restart the PC.
        Open a project directly: http://localhost:7870/?p=<project name>

FAST WAY
  Type the topic, paste your notes, pick voice/format/music, press
  "Make the whole video". Script, voice and MP4 are made in one go (3 to 6 minutes).
  If Google is busy, it waits and retries on its own for about 2 minutes.
  If it still stops, press "Try again": finished parts are kept.

CAREFUL WAY
  "Write script only", then check each scene before voicing and rendering.

EDITING A SCENE
  Camera shows:      বাংলাদেশ, মিয়ানমার       (or Bangladesh, Myanmar)
  Colour countries:  বাংলাদেশ=সবুজ, ভারত=নীল  (লাল কমলা সবুজ নীল ফিরোজা বেগুনি হলুদ গোলাপি ধূসর)
  Arrows:            চীন > মিয়ানমার : পাইপলাইন
  Points:            কক্সবাজার, 21.43, 91.97   (name, latitude, longitude)
  The chips under each scene show a flag when a country is recognised, a red ✗ when not.
  The picture on the right is a live preview of where the camera ends up. Use it to catch
  wrong coordinates (Gemini sometimes puts ports in the wrong place).

  Scene types: map, title, stat, bullets, timeline (date | text), compare (label | A | B), quote.

OWN AUDIO (when Gemini voice fails, or you prefer ElevenLabs / your own voice)
  Step 1 -> "🎧 I have my own audio".
  Choose the audio file, paste the EXACT script you recorded, with an empty line
  between scenes (one paragraph = one scene). Press "Build video from my audio".
  - Your words are never changed. Gemini only designs the maps; if Gemini is busy,
    automatic maps show the countries each paragraph mentions.
  - Whisper listens to the recording to find where each scene starts and to time
    the captions. Most cuts land within a fraction of a second, but a few can be
    off by a few seconds where Whisper mishears Bangla.
  - To fix a cut: change "starts at" (seconds) on that scene, press "Re-cut my audio",
    then Render. Play each scene's clip to check.
  - Tip for perfect cuts: when recording, leave about 1 second of silence between
    paragraphs.
  - "Generate audio" never overwrites your recorded scenes.
  - Section markers such as **[India — 0:20–0:55]** or # headings are fine: they are
    treated as headings (used as on-screen headlines), not as speech. ** bold marks are removed.
  - Short lines are joined into scenes of about 6-15 seconds automatically.
  - Lines that are in the script but NOT in the recording are detected and left out;
    the log names them. Paste the script that matches what was actually read.

AFTER EDITING
  Generate audio (only changed scenes are re-recorded), then Render video.

RENDER OPTIONS
  16:9 or 9:16, channel name (used in intro, corner and outro), music, captions,
  whoosh sounds, intro, outro.
  Music "✨ Auto" (default) picks a track that fits the story: refugees -> Heartbreaking,
  war -> Impact Lento, trade/ports -> Sovereign Quarter, spies -> Covert Affair, history -> Echoes of Time...
  The log says which track and why. Pick a track by hand to override.
  Every render with music saves <video>_CREDITS.txt: paste it in the YouTube description (licence requires it).
  More music: put .mp3 files in the music\ folder (add keywords in music\credits.json for Auto to use them).

NOTES
  Captions are timed to the real pauses in the voice.
  Renders use 12 CPU threads. GPU rendering was tested on this PC and was slower, so it is off.
  Map borders are Natural Earth de facto lines. Check disputed borders before publishing.
  Check every number and date Gemini writes.


===== NEW IN VERSION 2 =====
CHANNEL: pick it in the Channel dropdown (step 1 or step 4). "+" adds a new name, "x" removes one.
LOOK: Classic (navy), HUD (dark grid, red accent, corner labels like the reference videos), Light editorial.
FONTS: step 4 has two pickers, one for titles/cards and one for captions. Every Bangla font installed on the
  PC is listed (your usual ones at the top). To add a font like Purno, copy its .ttf into geo-explainer\fonts
  and restart the studio.
NEW SCENE TYPES (Gemini uses them automatically; you can also choose them per scene):
  globe       rotating 3D globe that turns to the countries in "Camera shows"
  choropleth  countries shaded by a number: one line per country, e.g.  চীন = 24.1
  chart       drawn in R: bar, hbar, lollipop, line, area, stacked, donut, pie, waffle, treemap.
              Data one line per point:  ২০২১ | 24.8   (add "| series" for several lines)
  broll       real stock footage from Pexels. Write an English search, e.g. "container port cranes".
              Needs a Pexels key (Settings), otherwise the Avatar Studio key is used.
EXTRA FILES next to every video: .srt captions, _YOUTUBE.txt (title, chapters, sources, music credit)
  and _thumbnail.jpg.
OWN AUDIO: your script is now matched to the recording word by word (forced alignment on the GPU),
  so scene cuts and captions follow your voice much more closely than before.

===== NEW IN VERSION 3: ANIMATION, MUSIC, SOUND EFFECTS =====
CHARTS: R now draws every frame, so each chart type builds up differently: bars rise one by one with
  counting numbers, rankings slide in from the top, lines draw themselves, donut/pie sweep round, stacked
  bars build layer by layer, waffle fills tile by tile, treemap blocks appear one by one.
  "stat" cards count up; a percentage (e.g. ৬৮%) also fills a ring.
SPREAD MAPS (scene type "spread"): something spreading inside a country.
  What:  flood, conflict, protest, fire, drought, disease, control, cyclone (each has its own colour/texture)
  How:   "District by district" (lights up in order, nearest to the start first)
         or "Flowing out from the start point" (an organic patch that stays inside the districts you list)
  Districts: Bangla or English, old or new spelling (Chattogram/Chittagong, Cumilla/Comilla...).
  Cyclone: add a path (lat, lon per line) and a storm icon travels along it.
  Boundaries come from geoBoundaries (BBS/OCHA); the credit is added to _YOUTUBE.txt automatically.
  Other countries work too: their provinces are downloaded the first time you use them.
MUSIC: 21 new tension tracks (war drums, dark orchestra, tense guitar) + sombre and victory tracks.
  "Auto" picks from the mood and never repeats the track of your last few videos.
SOUND EFFECTS: rain, storm, thunder, river, flood, sea, wind, war, explosion, bomb, gunshot, gunfire, fire,
  protest crowd, angry crowd, cheering, press, marching army, war drums, war horn, fanfare, siren, police,
  alarm, helicopter, heartbeat, drum roll, riser, impact, clock, drone.
  Automatic: chosen from the narration words (বন্যা -> flood, গুলি -> gunfire, বিজয় -> fanfare ...).
  Manual: type them in a scene's "Sound effects" box, e.g.  rain, gunfire@2.5  (@ = seconds into the scene).
  Licences: Mixkit (free, no credit needed) and BigSoundBank (CC0).
